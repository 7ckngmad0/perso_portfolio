About

A data-minded developer from Laguna

I'm an IT student from San Pedro, Laguna, studying at PUP's College of Computer and Information Sciences. My focus is data analytics: taking raw records like sales logs, survey results and messy spreadsheets, and turning them into something people can actually read and act on.

Most of my projects mix two things I enjoy: building web apps with Python and Flask, and making data understandable with charts and dashboards. I've also worked on networking labs in Cisco Packet Tracer and a Chrome extension backed by Firebase.

Right now I'm taking on freelance data-cleaning work and building Lectern, my own app for storing lecture notes.
